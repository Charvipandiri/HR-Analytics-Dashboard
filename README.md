# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This HR Analytics Dashboard was built using Tableau to analyze employee attrition trends, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, and education-based employee insights.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of employee data and helps identify factors contributing to attrition within the organization.

---

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Metrics
- Total Employee Count
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Employee Age
- Active Employee Count

### Attrition Analysis
- Department-wise Attrition
- Education Field-wise Attrition
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition Rate by Gender Across Different Age Groups

### Workforce Demographics
- Employee Distribution by Age Group
- Age Bin Filtering
- Education-based Filtering

### Employee Satisfaction
- Job Satisfaction Rating by Job Role
- Satisfaction Comparison Across Departments

## Key Insights

### Employee Overview
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Active Employees: 1,233
- Attrition Count: 237
- Average Employee Age: 37 Years

### Attrition Trends
- Sales department has the highest attrition.
- Research & Development follows as the second-highest contributor.
- HR has the lowest attrition count.

### Education Analysis
- Life Sciences employees show the highest attrition.
- Medical and Marketing fields contribute significantly to employee turnover.

### Gender Analysis
- Male attrition is higher than female attrition.
- Attrition patterns vary across age groups.

### Job Satisfaction
- Job satisfaction levels differ across job roles.
- Sales Executive and Research Scientist roles have the highest employee counts.
